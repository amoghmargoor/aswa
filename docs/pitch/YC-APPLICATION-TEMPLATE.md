### What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does.

Luemyn is an enterprise AI platform for legacy modernization. We have two integrated products:

**Luemyn Code** - A coding agent that understands legacy languages (COBOL, RPG, PL/I) and converts them to modern languages (Java). Unlike pattern-matching transpilers, we use AI to understand the semantics of legacy code and produce idiomatic, maintainable modern code.

**ASWA Platform** - An intelligence and agent platform that ingests enterprise documents (Jira, Confluence, Slack, PDFs). Users build custom AI agents using natural language - "When a customer email comes in, summarize it, check our knowledge base, and create a ticket."

The integration: Luemyn Code pulls context from ASWA. When converting `CALC-INTEREST-RATE`, it can find the Jira ticket that explains why that function exists, the compliance document it satisfies, and the Slack thread where the original decision was made.

We deploy on-premise and air-gapped, which is why banks and government agencies that can't use cloud AI are talking to us.

### What's new about what you're making? What substitutes do people resort to today?

**What's new:**
1. **Code + Context Integration**: We're the only platform that combines legacy code understanding with document intelligence. Everyone else does one or the other.

2. **Deployment Flexibility**: Full on-premise and air-gap support. Glean, Claude, GPT—all SaaS-only. Banks can't use them.

### Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don't?

**Competitors:**
- **Glean** ($4.6B): Best enterprise search, but read-only. No agents, no on-prem, no code.
- **Accenture/Deloitte**: Services companies. They make money on long projects; we make them obsolete.
- **Micro Focus**: Legacy tools for legacy code. No AI.
- **Anthropic/OpenAI**: Won't go on-premise, won't specialize in COBOL.

**What we understand:**
1. **Context is the product**: Every failed modernization project we've studied failed because code was converted without understanding WHY it was written that way. The documents matter as much as the code.

2. **Deployment is a feature**: For our target customers, on-premise isn't a nice-to-have—it's a legal requirement. Building for this from day one gives us a market competitors can't easily enter.

3. **Agents unlock expansion**: Once customers have their documents in our platform, they'll build agents for everything—support tickets, compliance monitoring, onboarding. The code conversion is the wedge; the agent platform is the expansion.

## Tips for YC Application

1. **Be concise**: Every word should earn its place
2. **Be specific**: Numbers > adjectives. "$50K pilot signed" > "great customer traction"
3. **Be honest**: YC invests in founders, not perfect stories
4. **Show velocity**: What have you done since you started? What will you do next week?
5. **Explain like to a smart friend**: No jargon, no buzzwords
6. **Differentiate clearly**: Don't just say you're better—explain the structural advantage
7. **Show market insight**: What do you know that others don't?
